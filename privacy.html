Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This explains what data ATM Assistant collects, why, and how it's handled. ATM Assistant is operated by Toheeb Akanni, based in Nigeria.

1. Information We Collect

DataWhenWhy
Email addressSigning up (email/password or Google)Account login, welcome email
Name & profile photoSigning up with GooglePersonalizing your account display
Chat messagesAny time you send a messageGenerating a response; saved to your history if logged in
IP addressSent with every messageEnforcing the free guest message limit and preventing abuse
Uploaded files/imagesIf you attach one to a messageIncluded in that message's request to the AI model

2. How We Use It

We use this data solely to operate the Service: authenticating you, generating AI responses, saving your chat history if you have an account, enforcing usage limits, and — if you sign up — sending you a welcome email through our CRM provider.

3. Who We Share It With

We don't sell your data. To operate the Service, some data is processed by these third parties:

  • Groq — processes your chat messages to generate AI responses
  • Supabase — stores your account, chat history, and settings
  • Google — if you choose "Continue with Google," handles that sign-in
  • Brevo — stores your email for welcome/marketing communications if you sign up

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing the data they process on our behalf.

4. Data Retention

Guest usage data (IP + message count) is tracked daily and reset each day. Account data (chats, settings) is kept until you delete your account or request removal. Guest chat sessions are never saved — they exist only in your browser and disappear when you close or reload the page.

5. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. If you're in Nigeria, this is consistent with your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser storage (via Supabase) to keep you logged in between visits. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions or data requests: toheeb@toheebakanni.name.ng
