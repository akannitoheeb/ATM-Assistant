Frequently Asked Questions

Is ATM Assistant free to use?

Yes. The Free plan includes 25 messages a day, single-email campaign generation, and the deliverability checklist. A Pro plan (₦8,000/mo or $9/mo) adds landing pages, SMS/social repurposing, multi-email sequences, unlimited brand profiles, and a higher daily limit.

Does ATM Assistant check my emails for spam triggers?

Yes. Every generated campaign is checked against common spam-trigger phrases, all-caps subject lines, missing unsubscribe language, and region-specific compliance requirements for CAN-SPAM (US), GDPR (EU), and CASL (Canada).

Can it write a full email sequence, not just one email?

Yes. Sequence mode generates a 3 to 5-email drip flow with realistic send-time pacing, where each email has a distinct purpose in the overall sequence.

How is ATM Assistant different from ChatGPT or Claude?

ATM Assistant is a general-purpose AI chatbot that can discuss any topic, like ChatGPT or Claude, but it has deep, practical expertise in email marketing specifically — subject lines, segmentation, automation flows, deliverability, and conversion copy — and outputs structured, ready-to-send campaigns rather than plain text.

Who built ATM Assistant?

ATM Assistant was built by Toheeb Akanni, a freelance email marketing strategist and WordPress web designer based in Lagos, Nigeria, operating under the brand ATM (Akanni Toheeb Marketing). See his portfolio at toheebakanni.name.ng.

Is my data kept private?

Chats and settings are saved to your account when you sign up, so you can pick up past conversations later. Guests can try the assistant without an account, with a limited daily message allowance and no saved history.
